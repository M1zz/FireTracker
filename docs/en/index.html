Toesagak

Track your assets, net worth and FIRE (early retirement) goal at a glance

About

Toesagak is a personal finance tracker. Add what you own and what you owe, and it works out your total assets and net worth. Log a snapshot each month to see how things change over time and how close you are to your FIRE (financial independence) goal. All data is stored only on your device, and there is no sign-up.

Key features

  • 📊Track assets by category (stocks, crypto, real estate, cash, pensions, debts and more) with details for each holding
  • 📈Monthly charts of assets, debts and net worth, plus the change since your last snapshot
  • 🎯FIRE progress based on your target spending and withdrawal rate, with a year-end projection
  • 📡Enter a ticker or stock code to fetch prices automatically for US stocks, Korean stocks, crypto and Korean real estate
  • 🖼️Add holdings in one go from a screenshot of your brokerage app, recognized entirely on your device

FAQ

Where is my financial data stored?

Only on your device (Apple SwiftData). Nothing is uploaded to a server, and the developer can’t see it.

Prices aren’t updating automatically.

US stocks, crypto and exchange rates work without any key. Korean stock and real estate prices need the matching API key (Korea Investment & Securities, or Korea’s Public Data Portal) entered in the Settings tab. Please also check your network connection and that the ticker or stock code is correct.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app deletes all data stored on your device along with it.

What’s the difference between total assets and net worth?

In the app, “total assets” is the sum of everything you own, and “net worth” is what’s left after subtracting your debts (total assets − debts).
